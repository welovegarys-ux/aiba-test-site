About this page

This page tests AIBA's website chat. Your message goes to an automation (Make.com), which checks it against approved business facts and the AIBA policy rules before anything is shown back.

  • Only approved facts (such as opening hours) are answered automatically.
  • Prices, bookings, complaints, urgent or clinical questions and anything unclear are passed to the team instead.
  • Nothing is booked, quoted or charged from this chat.

Privacy notice (test)

Who: the AIBA project team, testing with a fictional business. What: the name, email and message you send, and a random chat session code kept in this browser tab. Why: to answer your message and to test the service. Where: Make.com (EU), Supabase (database) and the AI model used by Make; a contact record may be created in the test CRM. How long: test data is deleted when testing ends. Your choices: do not send real health or personal information; to ask for your data to be deleted, say so in the chat.

This is not a real dental practice and cannot give dental advice. In an emergency call 999, or 111 for urgent advice.
